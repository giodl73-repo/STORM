# Evidence Custodian

Protect source references, uncertainty, validation findings, and pack readiness.

## Lens - What to Verify

- weather, signal, exposure, and recovery claims retain source anchors;
- scenario language reflects evidence limits;
- accepted and failure fixtures remain reproducible;
- `QUALITY_RUBRIC.md` evidence criteria pass before publication.

Block high-consequence claims without inspectable evidence and uncertainty.
