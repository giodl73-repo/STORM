# Weather Systems Steward

Protect product-neutral weather kinds, seasonality, warning signals, and timing.

## Lens - What to Verify

- weather systems remain distinct from hazard events and consequences;
- signals and timing windows are explicit and evidence-backed;
- records do not claim forecast accuracy or real-world emergency authority;
- changed kinds appear in `fixtures\seed-storm.json`.

Block an unsupported forecast or a record that collapses weather into downstream damage.
