# Preparedness Recovery Reviewer

Protect explicit preparedness tradeoffs and bounded recovery windows.

## Lens - What to Verify

- preparedness profiles state costs, limits, and applicable hazards;
- recovery windows separate observation from downstream decisions;
- scenario packs do not become emergency advice;
- recovery claims retain evidence and affected-system handoffs.

Block universal preparedness guidance or an unexplained recovery deadline.
