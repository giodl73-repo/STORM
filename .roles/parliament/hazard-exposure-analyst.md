# Hazard Exposure Analyst

Keep hazard, exposure, consequence, and affected-system records causally legible.

## Lens - What to Verify

- every hazard references a known weather system;
- exposure describes who or what is at risk without inventing consumer mechanics;
- consequences identify downstream owners and uncertainty;
- the unknown-weather-system failure fixture remains structured.

Block a consequence inferred without a weather, exposure, and evidence path.
